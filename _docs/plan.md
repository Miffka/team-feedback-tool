# Weekly Team Feedback Tool — v1 Scope

## Contributors
All team members contribute feedback.

## Format
Start / Stop / Continue cards. No limit on number of cards per person.

## Identity
Names appear by default. Contributors can opt in to anonymity on a per-card basis.

## Board Lifecycle
Boards are manually started by the facilitator. No automatic weekly trigger in v1.

## Pre-Reveal Visibility
Before the reveal, each person can only see their own cards.

## Reveal
The facilitator triggers a reveal. All cards appear simultaneously.

## Clustering
After the reveal, the team groups similar cards together.

## Prioritization
Dot voting: each person gets 3 votes, and can stack multiple votes on a single topic.

## Discussion Output
Decisions and action items are recorded as plain notes. No owner or due-date fields in v1.

## Meeting Record
The facilitator can upload audio, video, or a transcript after the meeting. No built-in recording capture in v1.

---

## Explicitly Out of Scope (v1)
- Automatic/scheduled board creation
- Built-in audio/video recording
- Action item owners, due dates, or task-tracking integration
- Card submission limits

## Open Questions for Later Iterations
- Should action items roll forward into the next week's board?
- Should there be a way to search/filter past boards?
